Our Community

Guidelines for a respectful, positive and connected community.

Kulzzy Radio Network is a community where people come together to celebrate lives, share moments, connect with others and enjoy great radio. These guidelines help us keep the community safe, welcoming and enjoyable for everyone.

Read Our Guidelines ↓

Welcome to the Kulzzy Community

Whether you are a listener, celebrant, business owner, supporter, creator or visitor, you are welcome here. Our community is built around respect, positivity, celebration and meaningful connection.

Community Guidelines

A few simple rules help us keep Kulzzy Radio Network enjoyable for everyone.

🤝

1. Respect Everyone

Treat listeners, presenters, celebrants, businesses and other community members with respect. Personal attacks, bullying, harassment, intimidation and deliberate attempts to embarrass others are not permitted.

❤️

2. Keep It Positive

Kulzzy Radio Network is built around celebration, entertainment, information and positive community engagement. Help us create an environment that lifts people up rather than bringing them down.

🚫

3. No Spam

Do not flood our community with repeated messages, links, promotions or advertisements. Businesses interested in advertising and sponsorship should use the appropriate Kulzzy Radio Network channels.

🔐

4. Respect Privacy

Never publicly share another person's private information, phone number, address, private conversations, passwords or photographs without permission.

🎂

5. Birthday & Celebration Messages

We love celebrating birthdays, achievements and special occasions. Please submit genuine and appropriate information. Do not use celebration features to impersonate people, submit malicious messages or deliberately spread false information.

👤

6. No Impersonation

Do not pretend to be Kulzzy Radio Network, a presenter, staff member, celebrant, business owner or another community member. Official announcements come through authorized Kulzzy Radio Network channels.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

7. Keep Content Appropriate

Content submitted to our platforms should be suitable for a broad audience. Sexually explicit material, threats, extreme abusive content, hate-based content and material encouraging illegal activity are not permitted.

✓

8. Be Honest

Do not deliberately submit false information, fake announcements, fraudulent promotions or misleading claims about another person or business. Share information responsibly.

📢

9. Business & Promotions

We welcome legitimate businesses and entrepreneurs. Promotional content should be submitted through the appropriate Kulzzy Radio Network advertising and promotional channels rather than repeatedly posted in community spaces.

⚠️

10. Report Problems

If you encounter abusive, inappropriate, misleading or suspicious content, report it to the Kulzzy Radio Network team rather than starting unnecessary arguments.

🛡️

11. Community Moderation

Kulzzy Radio Network may review, remove or restrict content or participation that violates these guidelines. Serious violations may result in immediate action.

🌟

12. Be Part of Something Positive

Every listener and community member contributes to the atmosphere of Kulzzy Radio Network. Share kindness, celebrate others, support legitimate businesses and help make our community a place people want to return to.

Together We Celebrate

From birthday celebrations to business promotions, achievements, special announcements and everyday moments, Kulzzy Radio Network exists to give people a reason to connect and celebrate.

🛡️ Our Moderation Promise

We want everyone to feel comfortable participating in the Kulzzy community. Content that seriously violates our guidelines may be removed, and accounts or participants may be restricted from community features where necessary.


Our team may take action when content threatens the safety, privacy, dignity or positive atmosphere of the community.

🎙️❤️

Kulzzy Radio Network is more than a radio platform. We are building a community where people can be heard, celebrated, informed and connected.
